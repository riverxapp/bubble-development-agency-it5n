FAQ

Common questions

Why Bubble instead of a custom codebase?

Bubble lets you launch dramatically faster and cheaper while keeping full flexibility — you can always export or rebuild later if you outgrow it. For most startups, speed to market wins.

Do we own the app?

Yes. From day one you have full ownership and access to your app, its database, and your Bubble workspace. We're your partner, not a gatekeeper.

How long does a typical build take?

An MVP is typically live in 4–6 weeks, and a full product in 8–12 weeks. We agree on a milestone schedule up front and stick to it.

Can you integrate with my existing tools?

Almost always. We regularly work with Stripe, HubSpot, Zapier, Make, OpenAI, custom APIs, and more. If it has an API, we can connect it.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes post-launch support. After that, most clients move to a Growth & Care retainer for ongoing improvements, monitoring, and new features.
